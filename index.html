I like programming the parts of a game most players never think about — the vision system that decides whether a guard sees you, the ring buffer that keeps audio from glitching, the render contract that stops a GPU state leak before it happens. Software architecture and design patterns are the throughline across everything I build: RAII, Strategy, Observer, Template Method, generational handles — not as buzzwords, but as the actual tools I reach for to keep systems decoupled and extensible.

My recent work spans a from-scratch DirectX 11 rendering engine, a multithreaded XAudio2 audio engine, a solo stealth game with a fully data-driven AI stack, and leading a small team through Unreal Engine stealth mechanics at Wispy Moon Studios. I care as much about whether a system is easy for someone else to extend as whether it works today.
